🚩 4 Badi Scam Pehchanein (Online Fraud se Kaise Bachein):

⏰

1. Farzi Jaldbazi & Darr (Fake Urgency)

Scammer kya bolta hai: "Aapka SBI account ya bijli connection 24 ghante me band ho jayega, turant is link par click karke update karein!"

💡 Asli Baat: Bank ya bijli vibhag bina written notice ke achanak account ya bijli nahi kat-ta. Kabhi panic me aakar link na kholein.
🔑

2. OTP, PIN ya Password Mangna

Scammer kya bolta hai: "Aapka ₹5,000 ka reward/refund pass ho gaya hai, confirm karne ke liye mobile par aaya OTP ya UPI PIN batayein."

💡 Golden Rule: Bank ka Manager ya Police bhi kabhi OTP ya ATM PIN nahi maang sakti. OTP kisi ke baap ko bhi na batayein!
🌐

3. Nakli Lookalike Website Links

Scammer kya bhejta hai: Asli website paypal.com ya onlinesbi.sbi hoti hai, jabki scammer paypa1-login.xyz jaisi nakli link bhejta hai.

💡 Asli Baat: Link par click karne se pehle spelling dhyaan se dekhein. Scammers 'o' ki jagah '0' ya 'l' ki jagah '1' use karte hain.
📷

4. Farzi QR Code (Quishing Scam)

Scammer kya bolta hai: "Ye QR code scan karo aur aapke account me turant ₹500 aa jayenge!"

💡 Sabse Badi Baat: QR Code sirf paise BHEJNE ke liye hota hai! Paise LENE (receive karne) ke liye kabhi QR scan nahi karna padta.

🛡️ Agar Galti se Link par Click ho gaya ya OTP chala gaya, toh turant kya karein:

1
Password Badal Dein: Turant official portal par jakar apna NetBanking, Email, aur UPI password badal dein.
2
Bank ko Call karke Card/Account Freeze Karein: Bank ke official toll-free number par call karein aur apna ATM card aur netbanking turant block karwayein.
3
1930 Helpline par Complaint Karein: Golden Hour (fraud ke 2-3 ghante ke andar) 1930 par complaint darj karane se bank transaction rok sakta hai aur aapka paisa bach sakta hai.

🚩 Critical Phishing Vectors & Defensive Tactics:

Coercive Time Urgency

Adversarial messages threatening imminent account deactivation or legal enforcement within 24 hours to bypass deliberate human validation.

Credential & OTP Harvesting

Deceptive prompts demanding second-factor OTP tokens, card verification codes, or master authentication passwords.

Typosquatted Lookalikes

Punycode, homoglyphs, and subdomains mimicking verified corporate entities (e.g., paypa1.com).

Quishing Physical Vectors

Malicious QR overlays on invoices or parking checkouts designed to bypass gateway security filters.

Enterprise Incident Response Protocol:

  1. Isolate endpoint device from enterprise network immediately upon link interaction.
  2. Revoke active session tokens and enforce global credential rotation on identity providers.
  3. Submit malicious artifact to SOC / ThreatGuard AI and file report on CERT-In / 1930 portal.
